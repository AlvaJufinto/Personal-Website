## Back to the topic.

Have you ever like your prayer doesn't get answered even though you feel lke praying "hard" enough. Yep, that was me a year before. I was overdemanding without exactly understood how faith really worked back then. After I contemplate, I think that is the fact: I only asked or prayed hard enough, or practiced to be a good Catholic if I have a wish. And the problem was: I only asked for worldly gift, nothing related to be more faithful or salvation. 

> "But seek first His kingdom and His righteousness, and all these things will be given to you as well."<br/> - Matthew 6:33

Everything changed when I asked to be more faithful and always be avoided from temptation. Then, I started to view it from a different perspective. Rather than chasing frivolously worldly gift, I'm so grateful for everything that God has given or taken from me, because everything leads to salvation of my soul. I don't complain no more even though things don't turn out like how I planned it.

I spent (not very a lot of time) in the adoration room for the last few weeks. I was praying in the adoration room, an hour before I got an email from ADA about my second chance. I didn't ask/complaining anything about ADA back then, just prayed and spent an hour with God. God really works in a mysterious way.

<br />

<Image src={Adoration} alt="Adoration" />

<br />

Same thing happened, a day before the acceptance announcement. It is thursday and I couldn't wait for the result. So instead of just overthinking, I went to the adoration room and recited the same prayer for over 77x times: ***Our Father***. The experience changed how you understood prayer.  

It's just an example, I was going through the impossible because I changed the purpose of my faith and prayer, also started to spend more time with God (btw don't forget to ask for our Mother of God's intercession).

> In the end, you can ask, hope, or pray for something but it is not a transaction.
