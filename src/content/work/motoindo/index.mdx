## Overview

Identified gaps in motoindo’s communication and transformed them into a clear digital experience that explains who they are, what they offer, and why customers should trust them

<MarginNote id="creative-note-2" side="left" lead="02 / Symptom first" anchor="prev" arrowTop="10px">This overview leads with communication gaps instead of a stack, which is the right order: nobody buys Next.js, they buy people understanding what they do.</MarginNote>

## Responsibilities

- Led the project from technical planning to production deployment.
- Designed and implemented the frontend architecture using Next.js.
- Integrated Sanity Headless CMS to enable flexible and maintainable content management.
- Optimized the website structure and metadata for better search visibility and SEO performance.
- Collaborated directly with stakeholders to align the website structure and content with business goals.

<MarginNote id="creative-note-3" side="right" lead="03 / The other deliverable" anchor="prev" arrowTop="66px">Sanity is in this list for a reason. A company site that still needs its developer to publish a page is not finished work.</MarginNote>
